SammlerVitrine gives your favorite collectibles a place of their own. Record a name, brand or manufacturer, storage location, and personal notes. Add one optional photo to recognize each item.

Search all four text fields, edit entries, and keep the stories behind your collection. One JPEG or PNG photo is saved directly with each entry. The original image file stays unchanged.

Everything stays on your computer. No account or internet connection is required. Choose German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, or Portuguese (Brazil). Language changes take effect the next time you start the app.
